**Headline.** 398 good pairs: forecast MAE **9.15 m**, zero-change MAE **5.82 m**, forecast strictly closer on **10.8%** of pairs. Exact ties: 0.0%. Both errors ≤ 15 m: 79.4%.

## Spatial block holdout

Five contiguous blocks of 15 transects. For each block, the block plus two transects on each side are removed from training; the same frozen feature set and random-forest hyperparameters are refit with targets on or before 2023; 2024–2026 are forecast from origin 2023 and compared with zero change on the same good transects. There is no tuning and no bootstrap interval. This is post-hoc on years already scored and has very low power.

## Unresolved and caveats

- The supplied `observed_banklines` chordal polyline exceeds the 30 m boundary check when sampled densely; the displayed line is the actual mask boundary. See the boundary table.
- The 2024 left-bank `T070` point lies on a 6-pixel disconnected component; all components are retained and no bank was dropped.
- 52 of 450 pairs are flagged/invalid. They remain visible as hollow markers and are counted; they are not scored.
- The spatial holdout is post-hoc, uses years already scored, has five blocks of about 15 transects, and has no bootstrap interval. It is a sensitivity check, not a blind test.
- `evaluation/metrics.json` has no closer-share field. The overviews therefore take MAEs and n from metrics.json and compute closer/farther/ties from predictions.csv; the two sources agree on n and MAE.
- The 4 px 2023 line is measured in final image pixels at 200 dpi. The observed line is 2 px so the light-grey line remains visible underneath it; at the old equal width the grey would be hidden.
- The `error exaggerated 5x` panel multiplies only connector lengths. Its endpoints are illustrative and are not bank locations; the true-scale panel and all numeric checks remain true scale.
- No required input was missing. All metric checks are within 0.01 m. Frozen and model hashes were unchanged, and `/api/health/` passed.
